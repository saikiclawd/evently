Floral AI Stem Calculator

An AI-powered wedding floral tool that reads bouquet photos, centerpiece images, and Pinterest boards to produce accurate stem counts, budgets, and sourcing lists.

Brainstorm & Concept Document

1. The Problem We're Solving

📋

Manual Counting Is Error-Prone

Florists spend hours manually estimating stems from inspiration images, often over- or under-ordering by 20–40%, leading to waste or last-minute scrambles.

💸

Brides Lack Transparency

Couples receive a single total quote with little visibility into individual flower costs. They can't easily swap varieties to hit a budget target.

🖼️

Pinterest Boards Are Ambiguous

Inspiration images mix styles, seasons, and sizes. Translating them into a real shopping list is a professional skill most couples don't have.

📅

Seasonality & Availability

Certain blooms peak at specific times of year and vary wildly in cost. Brides rarely know which flowers in their inspiration are out of season.

→

→
📊 Output Report Full stem list, budget range, seasonal notes & substitutions

3. Image Input Types & How AI Handles Each

💐

Bridal Bouquet

Single arrangement, tightly framed. AI focuses on:

  • Focal flowers (roses, peonies, dahlias)
  • Secondary blooms (ranunculus, lisianthus)
  • Filler (baby's breath, wax flower)
  • Greenery (eucalyptus, Italian ruscus)
  • Rough stem count per group based on density
High Accuracy Single Image
🕯️

Centerpiece / Table Arrangement

Variable complexity: low garden clusters or tall statement pieces. AI handles:

  • Silhouette type detection (low/high/cascade)
  • Vessel size estimation from context cues
  • Stem density inference per tier
  • Repetition scaling: × number of guest tables
Medium Accuracy Scales by Table Count
📌

Pinterest Board / Collage

Multi-image grid with mixed styles. AI processes:

  • Each pin individually, then merges results
  • Detects dominant colour palette & aesthetic
  • Flags style conflicts (e.g., rustic vs modern)
  • Builds a "mood-weighted" stem list from all pins
  • Outputs a unified shopping list with variance ranges
Complex Input Aggregated Output
🏛️

Ceremony Installation

Arches, aisles, altar pieces, chuppah. AI uses:

  • Scale inference (door frame, human reference)
  • Coverage area estimation in sq ft
  • Stem-per-sq-ft density lookup by style
  • Installation type templates (A-frame, circle, half-moon)
Scale-Dependent Template Assist

5. AI Model Strategy

👁️

Vision Classification

Use a multimodal LLM (Claude claude-sonnet-4-6 or GPT-4o) with a structured prompt to:

  • List all detected flower species
  • Estimate relative proportion of each (% of arrangement)
  • Identify arrangement style (cascading, dome, garden, structured)
  • Note colour palette as hex or named swatches
📐

Stem Count Formula

Combine AI proportions with lookup tables:

  • focal_stems = base_count × focal_pct
  • base_count varies by arrangement type (bouquet ~25, centerpiece ~40–60)
  • Scale by event size multiplier from user inputs
  • Add 10–15% waste/breakage buffer automatically
🌱

Seasonality Engine

A lookup database per flower variety stores:

  • Peak season months (hemisphere-aware)
  • Off-season price premium multiplier
  • Top 3 substitutions by colour & form
  • Availability flag per user's event date
💬

Conversational Refinement

After the initial estimate, allow users to chat:

  • "Replace peonies with garden roses to save money"
  • "I want a more lush, overflowing look"
  • "Add a flower wall — 8ft × 8ft"
  • "My florist says ranunculus are hard to find locally"

7. Smart Feature Ideas

🎨

Colour Palette Extractor

Automatically pull the dominant 5-colour palette from the image and let users filter the stem list by matching hues, so substitutions stay on-brand.

Visual Branding
🔄

Budget Slider

Drag a total-budget slider and watch the AI automatically swap expensive focal flowers for affordable alternatives, keeping the look as close as possible to the original.

Interactive Budget Tool
📦

Wholesale Marketplace Link

One-click export to floral wholesale platforms (Mayesh, FiftyFlowers, BloomNation) with pre-filled cart items matching the stem count output.

Commerce Integration
🗓️

Ordering Timeline

Based on the wedding date, generate a backwards timeline: when to order, when blooms arrive, when to condition stems, and hydration day-of schedule.

Planning Logistics
🤝

Florist Collaboration Mode

Share a read-only link with the hired florist. They can annotate the AI estimate, adjust quantities, and attach their quote — all in one place.

Collaboration Pro Tool
📍

Local Grower Finder

After stem list is generated, surface nearby farms and local growers who carry those varieties — supporting local business and reducing shipping costs.

Local Sustainability
♿

Allergy & Sensitivity Filter

Flag flowers with strong scents or known allergens (lilies, stephanotis, freesia). Offer fragrance-free substitutions for sensitive guests.

Accessibility Health
🌍

Sustainability Score

Rate the arrangement's environmental footprint based on air-freight miles, pesticide use, and water consumption. Suggest a "greener" equivalent list.

Eco Branding

{
  "arrangement_type": "bouquet | centerpiece | arch | installation | mixed",
  "style": "garden | structured | cascade | tropical | wildflower | modern",
  "colour_palette": ["#hex1", "#hex2", ...],

    {
      "common_name": "Garden Rose",
      "botanical_name": "Rosa hybrida",
      "role": "focal | secondary | filler | greenery",
      "proportion_pct": 35,
      "confidence": 0.92
    }
  ],
  "notes": "string — any style or sourcing observations"
}

12. Open Questions & Next Steps

❓

Accuracy Validation

How do we validate stem counts against real florist quotes? Consider partnering with 5–10 florists to label training images and create a ground-truth dataset.

💰

Pricing Data Source

Flower wholesale prices fluctuate seasonally. Do we maintain our own pricing DB, scrape wholesalers, or use an existing floral pricing API?

📏

Scale Estimation

Without a reference object in image, scale is ambiguous. Should we ask users to describe arrangement size, or use AI to infer from context clues?

🎯

Target User

Is this primarily for brides planning DIY florals, or a B2B tool for professional florists to speed up their quoting workflow? The UX differs significantly.
